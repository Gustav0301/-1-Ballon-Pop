Sky Zone Layers

1+ Ballon Pop · how every zone looks

The idea

Every zone should feel like a new place. Not only new hazards: a new sky, new clouds around you, new light and new things flying past. You climb through it like layers of a cake.

Each zone is built from the same 6 layers, from far away to close to you:

SkySky colours, sun, fog and light.
FarScenery far away. You never reach it.
MiddleThe clouds around you.
NearSmall bits that fly past you.
BelowWhat you see when you look down.
TransitionThe last ~100 studs before the next zone.

How it works in the game

  • 1

    One list of sky settings. Each zone is one entry in one config file. A new zone just adds a new entry.

  • 2

    Smooth blend by height. Colours, fog and light mix slowly as you climb. No hard jump.

  • 3

    Your own sky. It runs on each player's screen (client side). Two players at different heights see different skies.

  • 4

    Stud clouds that follow you. Cloud layers are stud-style part models. They move with you sideways, so you never see their edge.

  • 5

    Zone banner + sound. When you enter a zone, its name pops in and a sound plays.

Weather (changes every 5 minutes) plays on top of the zone look. It never replaces it.

Roblox parts used: Lighting · Atmosphere · Sky · ColorCorrection · Clouds · ParticleEmitter · stud part models

Build order

Only Meadow Sky and Cloud Shelf are open now. Make those two perfect first. Build the others when their phase opens.

  1. 1Now: Meadow Sky + Cloud Shelf + the jump between them

    This is what every player sees first. It must feel amazing.

    Meadow SkyPuffy stud clouds, petals and pollen, the mountain ring far away. Add the 4 weathers on top.
    The punch-throughCloud ceiling at 400, thicker mist, a whoosh and a puff at 500, then golden light.
    Cloud ShelfThe cloud floor below you, tall cloud towers, golden haze, mist wisps and sparkle dust.
  2. 2Phase 2

    Kite Fields and Windways. Build when Phase 2 opens, with updrafts, kites and gust walls.

  3. 3Phase 3: Storm

    Thunderhead and Hail Belt. Dark storm look first, then the ice.

  4. 4Phase 4: Space

    Stratosphere and Orbit. Planet curve, stars, moons and the Moon Hub.

  5. +Every new zone

    Add the zone's entry to the sky list, its banner, its sound loop and its weathers.
