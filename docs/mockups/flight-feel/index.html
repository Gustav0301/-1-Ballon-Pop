Air DashAir DashNEW · SHIFT

Press Shift and your balloon squeezes, blows out a puff of air, and throws you sideways past a bird. It costs a little balloon size, and you sink a bit for a moment.

-6% sizeabout 24 studs2 s waitphone: blue button
  • 1Squeeze: the balloon squashes, rubber squeak
  • 2Puff: an air cloud shoots out the back, a ring of air, whoosh
  • 3You lean hard into the dash with your knees tucked, wind lines fly past
  • 4The balloon trails behind, then wobbles back. "-3 SIZE" pops up
  • 5A bird that just misses you: NICE DODGE!
FlyingFlyingNEW · ALWAYS ON

All four effects work together. Steering is a bit quicker and turning around is snappy, but you still drift.

  • aThe balloon stretches when it moves, wobbles when you turn, and bumps on every +1
  • bYour body banks into turns and leans forward with speed. Legs trail behind and swing
  • cWind lines and a wind sound when you go fast. The camera widens a little
  • dThe rope bends and swings behind you instead of a straight line
LandingLandingBIGGER

Your hero landing, but harder. You dive in, the game freezes for a split second as you hit, then plays slow-motion while the ground explodes.

  • 1Short hop up, then a fast dive onto the pad
  • 2Freeze for 0.1 s, then 0.3 s of slow-motion
  • 3Shock ring, dust, grass and dirt studs that bounce, camera shake
  • 4A coin fountain flies into your coin counter, which counts up and bumps
PopPopBIGGER

A pop should hurt (in a funny way). The balloon blows apart in its own colours and you flail all the way down.

  • 1Freeze for 0.1 s at the hit, then a bang and a big POP!
  • 2Rubber pieces in the balloon's colours fly off and spin
  • 3The rope goes loose and flutters from your hand
  • 4You wave your arms and kick your legs while you fall. Lost coins show in red
