BALLOON CRATES

One crate on each island, each better than the last. Drag a crate to turn it, and press OPEN to see the spin, the burst and the balloon you win.

300,000

Notes for building it in Studio

  1. Each crate stands on a stud plinth in front of a small stall, on its own island: Meadow at spawn, Cloud on Rainbow Shelf, Kite on Kite Harbor, Storm on Gust Gate.
  2. In the game, all four crates are built from Gustav's imported stud crate. The decorations here show the theme for each one.
  3. Walk up and hold F (like the island reward objects). The opening plays in the world, so players nearby see it, and the camera moves in a little.
  4. The prices and chances are first guesses and live in CrateConfig.
