# Sounds and music

## The sound pack (made for this game)

`sounds/` holds 20 original sound effects, generated by `tools/sounds/make_sounds.py`. They're synthesised from scratch, so they're free to use and you own them. `build/sounds.zip` has the same files in one download.

| Sound | When it plays |
|---|---|
| **Pop** | A balloon pops: a sharp crack, a thump, then a confetti sparkle. It plays in 3D, and bigger balloons pop deeper. |
| **Launch** | You take off: a whoosh plus a stretchy inflate. |
| **Tick** | A soft coin tick about once a second while you earn. Its pitch rises as the balloon grows. |
| **LetOut** | A loop of air hissing out while you hold Space. |
| **LandStart** | Landing started: a little rising pluck. |
| **Bank** | Coins cascade into the counter, ending in a sparkle chord. |
| **CoinIn** | Each coin of the banked fountain arriving. |
| **Max** | You hit max size: a bell chime. |
| **Hit** | A hazard hits your balloon: a thud plus a rubbery squeak. |
| **Fall** | A slide-whistle fall after a pop. |
| **Click / Open / Close** | Menu buttons and windows. |
| **Buy** | Ka-ching and bells. |
| **Upgrade** | A level-up arpeggio. |
| **Equip** | A boing. |
| **Error** | Not enough coins, sold out and so on. |
| **Teleport** | The top-bar teleports. |
| **Restock** | Ding-dong when the shop restocks. |
| **Fanfare** | A Legendary or Mythic is in stock. |

To change a sound, edit its function in `make_sounds.py` and run `python3 tools/sounds/make_sounds.py`.

## Putting them in the game

1. In Studio, open **View → Asset Manager**, click **Bulk Import**, and pick all the `.ogg` files from `sounds/`. You can also upload them one at a time on the Creator Hub under *Development Items → Audio*. Audio uploads are free, but there's a monthly limit.
2. When they're uploaded, right-click each one and choose **Copy Asset ID**.
3. Paste each ID into `src/shared/Config/SoundConfig.lua` (just the number is fine, or `rbxassetid://` plus the number). In the drag-in pack, that's `GamePack → Shared → Config → SoundConfig`:
   ```lua
   Pop = { Id = "rbxassetid://1234567890", Volume = 0.9, Vary = 0.1 },
   ```

Any sound whose ID is left empty stays silent, so you can add them one at a time. `Volume` is the loudness, and `Vary` randomises the pitch a little so repeated sounds don't feel robotic.

## Music

`SoundConfig` has two music slots: **MusicGround** (the start map) and **MusicSky** (while flying). The game crossfades between them as you take off and land. Good places to get music:

- **Roblox's own library.** In Studio, go to **Toolbox → Audio** and pick Music. Roblox has licensed a big catalogue that's free to use in any Roblox game. Look for tracks uploaded by *Roblox*. Something bright and bouncy for the ground and a floatier, dreamier track for the sky will suit the game.
- **BeepBox** ([beepbox.co](https://www.beepbox.co)). A free tool in your browser for making chiptune songs, and what you make is yours. Export it as WAV, convert it to OGG or MP3, and upload it.
- **Pixabay Music** ([pixabay.com/music](https://pixabay.com/music/)). Free music you can use without credit. Search for "happy", "ukulele" or "cartoon".
- **AI music generators** such as Suno and Udio. They're quick, but read the plan's rules first: on some free plans you can't use the songs in a game you make money from.

## More sound effects

- **Kenney** ([kenney.nl/assets](https://kenney.nl/assets?q=audio)). Free game sound packs (UI clicks, impacts, casino coins) that you can use for anything, no credit needed.
- **sfxr / jsfxr** ([sfxr.me](https://sfxr.me)) and **ChipTone** (by SFB Games). Free browser tools for making retro sound effects.
- **Freesound** ([freesound.org](https://freesound.org)). A huge library. Filter by the **Creative Commons 0** licence so you don't have to give credit.
- **Roblox's Toolbox → Audio → Sound Effects**, uploaded by *Roblox*. Free to use.
